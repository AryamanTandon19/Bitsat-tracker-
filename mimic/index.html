How to play

Hiders get 45 seconds to explore the park — museum, houses, gardens — strike a pose among the mannequins, and paint themselves to blend in. You can even repaint the mannequins (or copy their look). Then… freeze!

At dusk the Seeker patrols with a flashlight and taps statues to accuse them. Wrong guess = lose points. Move in the light and you flash red!

Glowing orbs are worth points — but you have to move to grab them. 😈
