How to play

Hiders get 40 seconds to walk around the museum and strike a pose among the mannequins. Then… freeze!

The Seeker patrols with a flashlight and taps statues to accuse them. Wrong guess = lose points. If you move while the light is on you, you flash red!

Glowing orbs are worth points — but you have to move to grab them. 😈
