# 🎯 Combat Rules — Strategic, Tekken-style

## Match
- 1 vs 1. Best of 3 rounds. ~60 sec per round.
- Win = drain enemy health bar, or more health when time ends.

## Health & meters
- **Health Bar** — you lose, you die.
- **Power Meter** — fills as you fight; spend it on **specials**.
- **Stun/Guard Meter** — block too much = guard break (strategy!).

## Buttons (simple set)
- **P** = Punch (fast, weak)
- **K** = Kick (slow, strong)
- **G** = Guard / Block
- **D** = Dash / Dodge (sidestep, the Tekken feel)
- **Specials** = button combos (e.g. ←→ + P)

## Every fighter has
- **4 Special Moves** (each costs power, each has a job):
  1. **Poke** — fast, safe, chip damage.
  2. **Launcher** — hits up, starts a combo.
  3. **Heavy/Grab** — big damage, slow, risky.
  4. **Signature/Ultimate** — themed super move, big meter cost.
- **Strength + Weakness** — so no fighter is best at all. Rock-paper-scissors balance.

## Stats per fighter (0–10)
- **Power** — hit damage
- **Speed** — move + attack speed
- **Range** — how far attacks reach
- **Defense** — damage taken
- **Tricky** — mind-games / mix-ups
> Rule: total stat points are EQUAL for all (balance). Strong here = weak there.

## Strategy layer
- Spacing (range control), baiting, punishing, meter saving.
- Stage matters: some stages have walls (wall combos) or open edges (ring-out style).
