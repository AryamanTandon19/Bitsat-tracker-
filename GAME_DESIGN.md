# 🎮 NETA LIFE — Game Design Document

> A meme open-world game about Indian politics.
> Style: **AAA realistic** (GTA-like). Tone: **funny + satire**.
> **All names, faces, parties are FAKE.** No real politician. Funny look-alikes only. This keeps us safe from law and still very meme.

---

## 1. ONE-LINE PITCH
You are a poor chai-wala. You climb from street to the top "Boss" chair of the country — by speeches, rallies, bribes, drama, and pure meme power.

---

## 2. THE PLAYER FANTASY
- Feel like a rising star neta in a living, breathing fake-India.
- Do funny political stuff in a serious-looking AAA world.
- Every action = consequence. Crowd loves you OR riots against you.

---

## 3. SETTING / WORLD
- One big open city: **"Nayi Dilli"** (fake capital) + nearby village + town.
- Living world: traffic, cows on road, street food, festivals, rain, day/night.
- Landmarks (parody): Big Gate, Round Parliament, River Ghat, Cricket Stadium.
- Districts have different vibe: slum, market, posh colony, farm belt.

---

## 4. MAIN STORY (acts)
1. **Chai to Charcha** — sell chai, give first street speech, get noticed.
2. **Party Time** — join a fake party, do dirty errands, build name.
3. **City Wars** — win district by district vs rival netas.
4. **State Boss** — become Chief of the state, betrayal arc.
5. **The Big Chair** — final campaign to become national "Boss".

---

## 5. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
1. Get mission from party / public.
2. Travel across open world.
3. Do action (speech, rally, race, sabotage, deal).
4. Gain **Vote Points + Money + Fame**, lose or gain **Trust**.
5. Spend on upgrades, unlock new district, repeat.

---

## 6. KEY STATS (the meters)
- **Votes** — main win currency.
- **Money** — buy cars, posters, bribes.
- **Trust** — break promise = down; deliver = up. Low trust = riots.
- **Fame** — unlock bigger rallies + cameos.
- **Heat** — news reporters & rivals chase you when too high.

---

## 7. PLAYER MOVEMENTS / ABILITIES (AAA feel)
- Walk, sprint, climb stage, sit, wave, namaste, garland throw.
- Drive: scooter, jeep, SUV convoy, tractor, even buffalo cart 🐃.
- **Speech mode** — rhythm/QTE mini game; hit beats = crowd roars.
- **Selfie & Dance** at rallies for fame combo.
- **Promise system** — pick promises on a wheel (risk vs reward).
- **Hide / Dodge reporters** stealth segments.

---

## 8. MISSION TYPES
- **Election** — win street/booth by crowd size.
- **Rally Boss Fight** — out-crowd the rival's rally in real time.
- **Convoy Race** — beat rival motorcade across city.
- **Sabotage** — steal rival's stage, leak rival's "meme".
- **Festival Save** — protect a festival event = big trust reward.
- **Debate Duel** — turn-based word battle with combo meter.

---

## 9. MEME / HUMOR LAYER
- Quotable fake one-liners ("Mitron..."-style, but original).
- Viral Moment system: after big win, game auto-makes a shareable meme card.
- Wardrobe: white kurta, big sunglasses, fake mustache, jacket of many colors.
- Funny side characters: Godi Reporter, Chacha Fixer, Aunty Voter, Troll Army.
- Radio stations with parody news + parody songs.

---

## 10. CHARACTERS (all FAKE)
- **You** — customizable rising neta.
- **Party Boss "Highcommand Ji"** — your shadowy mentor.
- **Rival "Vikas Verma"** — slick clean-image opponent.
- **Chacha Fixer** — gives you shady side gigs.
- **Godi Reporter** — friend or foe based on Heat.

---

## 11. PROGRESSION & UPGRADES
- Unlock districts as Votes rise.
- Upgrade: convoy, stage size, sound system, social-media farm, security.
- Skill tree: Speech, Charisma, Cunning, Strength, Wealth.

---

## 12. ART DIRECTION (AAA realistic)
- Realistic India lighting: dusty golden evenings, neon festival nights.
- High-detail crowds (lots of NPCs) = the signature look.
- Realistic faces but **clearly fictional** people.
- Reference vibe: GTA V city density + Indian street texture.

---

## 13. TECH PLAN
- **Engine: Unreal Engine 5** — best for AAA realism (Nanite, Lumen, big crowds).
  - Alt: Unity HDRP if team is more Unity-skilled.
- Crowd tech: mass AI / niagara for huge rally crowds.
- Target: PC first, then console.
- Source control: Git + Git LFS (big art files).

---

## 14. SCOPE — BE REAL 🪨
AAA is HUGE (big team, big time, big money). So we build in slices:
- **Vertical Slice (first goal):** ONE street, ONE rally, ONE speech mini-game, ONE rival. Make THAT feel great.
- Then grow district by district.

---

## 15. ROADMAP (slices)
1. **Prototype** — grey-box street, basic walk + speech mini-game.
2. **Vertical Slice** — one polished district, one full mission, one rival.
3. **Alpha** — full city, all mission types, story acts 1–2.
4. **Beta** — all acts, polish, meme systems, optimization.
5. **Launch** — PC release + viral meme marketing.

---

## 16. RISKS
- **Legal:** keep everything fictional + clear satire disclaimer.
- **Scope:** AAA realism is the #1 risk — protect the vertical slice.
- **Sensitivity:** punch at "the system", not at any real religion/community.
