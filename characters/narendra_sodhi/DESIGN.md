# 🧍 Character Design — NARENDRA SODHI (Fighter #1)

> Satire. Fully fictional look-alike for comedy only.

## 1. Identity
- **Name (fake):** Narendra Sodhi
- **Nickname / title:** "The Beard" / The Statesman
- **Country vibe (fake):** Indian statesman / South Asian political leader
- **Home stage:** Big Parliament 🏛️
- **One-line meme:** "Looks like he gives speeches that somehow become boss battles."

## 2. Personality
- Mood: Calm, composed, commanding.
- How he talks: Slow, grand, speech-like.
- Funny habit: Pauses dramatically before every hit.

## 3. Look (for Blender modeling)

### Face shape
- Oval leaning slightly square; broad tall forehead (~1/3 of face).
- Softly squared lower jaw, wide mandibular angle.
- Cheekbones moderate, softened by beard. Chin broad + rounded, projects moderately.
- Mature, symmetrical, minor natural asymmetry.

### Skin
- Fair to light-medium warm; slight pink on cheeks.
- Age texture: forehead lines, faint glabellar "11" lines, crow's feet, under-eye bags, nasolabial folds.

### Forehead
- Broad, tall. Receded hairline, shallow "M" front.
- Fine horizontal lines + one deeper mid-line.

### Eyebrows
- Medium-thick, white/grey, coarse hairs.
- Slight arch, denser inner ends, downward outer angle.

### Eyes
- Medium almond, slightly deep-set, hooded upper lids.
- Calm focused gaze, **directed slightly to HIS left**.
- Dark brown iris, ~one eye-width apart, mild under-eye bags + crow's feet.

### Nose
- Medium-large, straight bridge, high nasal root.
- Widens to a rounded tip, moderately wide nostrils, projects moderately.

### Beard + Mustache
- Full dense beard, white with light-grey shading, ~2–4 cm.
- Covers chin, jaw, sideburns, upper neck; rounded lower silhouette, follows jaw.
- Thick mustache merges into beard, covers most of upper lip.

### Hair
- Very short, white/grey, combed back. High hairline, even sides.

### Mouth
- Closed, neutral, slight downward rest. Thin upper lip, fuller lower lip (mostly hidden). No teeth.

### Ears
- Medium, slightly protruding, attached lobes. Top ≈ eyebrow height, bottom ≈ nose base.

### Marks / accessories
- **Rimless eyeglasses** (thin arms, no frame rims).
- No scar, mole, tilak, tattoo, piercing.

### Body
- Height ~170–178 cm (tall-ish). Medium build, mature.
- Moderately broad shoulders (~2 head-widths), chest medium depth, slight waist curve.
- Upright posture, weight slightly on one leg, feet turned slightly out, chin slightly lowered, head turned gently to his left.
- **Hands clasped/folded in front of lower torso** (signature pose).
- Average arms, medium hands, slightly thick fingers, short groomed nails.

### Clothes
- **Top:** Off-white/cream long kurta, full sleeves, mandarin collar, knee-length, straight fit.
- **Stole/scarf:** White with **saffron-orange decorative borders**, draped around neck (SIGNATURE meme item).
- **Bottom:** White churidar trousers, slim around calves/ankles.
- **Footwear:** Black open-toe sandals, two broad straps, flat sole.

### Color palette
- Off-white / Cream • White • Saffron Orange • Black (sandals)

### Signature prop
- **Long saffron-bordered stole** around the neck.

## 4. Fight Style
- Style name: "Statesman Stance"
- Stance: Upright, calm, defensive guard.
- Strength: **Power + Defense**; control space, punish mistakes.
- Weakness: **Slow speed**; struggles vs fast rushdown fighters.

## 5. Stats (total ≈ 30)
- Power: **8**
- Speed: **4**
- Range: **6**
- Defense: **8**
- Tricky: **4**

## 6. The 4 Special Moves
1. **Poke — "Mann Ki Jab"** · input `→ P` · quick straight palm jab, safe chip, good spacing.
2. **Launcher — "Stole Flick"** · input `↓↗ K` · flicks saffron stole upward, launches enemy for combo.
3. **Heavy / Grab — "Namaste Clap"** · input `→→ P+K` · unfolds clasped hands into a big two-palm clap-grab, high damage, slow.
4. **Signature / Ultimate — "Unity Wave"** · input `↓↓ P+K (4 meter)` · raises one hand, expanding energy wave pushes opponent back + big damage while he stays composed.
